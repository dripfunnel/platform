The upgrade brings the starting themes' loading skeletons to existing stores (#1598). The release notes gain codemods that rewrite each loading block of a Baseline, Classic or category theme still holding the shipped markup (cart, checkout, order, account, orders, wishlist, product, collection title, blog, content and the "Loading more products" line) into grey blocks shaped like the loaded page, with a band of the page colour sweeping across them every 1.6 seconds, and add the skeleton classes and the `shimmer` keyframes to the theme's styles (`theme.css`; Classic: its shop and product styles). Core's reduced-motion rule leaves the blocks plain, and a theme that draws its own loading states is left as it is. No change to the package's behaviour or API. Major because every store's look changes (docs/code/ARCHITECTURE.md §5), so it reaches a live store at its merchant's next publish. It replaces the two patch changesets #1599 and #1603 added.
