A password `TextField` shows and hides what was typed (#1731). Give it `showLabel` and `hideLabel` (the theme's "Show password" and "Hide password") and a button with an eye icon appears inside the box, on the right; the password starts hidden. Without the two words the field is as before. The starting themes pass them on Create an account, Sign in and Reset password.
