Do and don't: orange contrast

Brand orange is the action colour, and its label is always white. It is never a text colour on paper: use orange deep #B8541F for that.

Do

Orange fill with a white label — the one primary button.

Orange deep for link text on light surfaces.

#B8541F on white — 4.8:1.

Badge

Orange as rules, icons and tint fills.

The same orange fill carries onto ink — one CTA colour everywhere.

Don't
Get a proposal

Never a tinted or faded orange behind the white label — the label disappears. The fill is #EC844F at full strength or it is not a primary button.

Never brand orange as body text on white.

2.63:1, fails WCAG AA.

Never orange text on navy at body sizes.

2.98:1, fails. Eyebrow labels only, and only at 12px letter-spaced.

Never let orange exceed 5% of a page. It should point at one thing, not decorate everything.

12

Logo usage

The lockup is a two-tone wordmark — “Drip” in navy, “Funnel” in orange — beside the orange D-funnel mark. Clear space on all sides equals the cap height of the D. Never recolour, rotate, outline or stretch the lockup, and never rebuild it from live type.

Navy and orange. For paper, white and any light surface. Dashed rule marks the minimum clear space, not part of the artwork.

White and orange. For ink backgrounds and photography dark enough to hold white type.

Clear space and sizing
1x cap height of the D on every side, measured from the artwork bounding box.
Minimum 120px wide on screen, 24mm in print. Below that, use the mark on its own.
Mark on its own
Favicons, app icons, avatars and any space under 120px. Keep the mark orange on both light and ink. Vector SVG for the mark and lockup, plus 512px raster app icons on paper and ink.
Favicon · round
Light — white ground, navy F
Dark — ink ground, white F
The mark is fitted by its bounding-box diagonal at 80% of the diameter, so the square corner of the top bar keeps the same margin from the rim as the round of the D. Shipped at 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, 180, 256 and 512px in both modes — a browser tab picks the mode with prefers-color-scheme, never a single file for both. The 16px art is drawn at 16, not downscaled from 512.
Contrast note
The orange “Funnel” inside the lockup sits below AA as text would — logos are exempt from WCAG, so this is correct in the artwork and nowhere else. Never set headings or body copy to match it.
Never add a stroke, container or drop shadow to make the lockup work on a busy background. Move the background instead.

Softobotics Group endorsement

DripFunnel is the customer-facing brand; Softobotics is the parent. The parent is credited in type, never as a second logo beside ours — two marks side by side reads as a partnership, not ownership. Three permitted treatments, in order of how prominent the relationship should be.

A · Endorsed lockup
Part of the Softobotics Group

Descriptor set in Inter 500, 12px, +10% tracking, text muted, left edge aligned to the D. Orange hairline separates it from the lockup. For pitch decks, proposal covers, contracts and email signatures.

B · Footer credit
DripFunnel is a Softobotics Group company. Softobotics Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

The default for the website. Sits in the ink footer above the legal line, Inter 400 at 14px in ink soft, with the registered entity name in white. Never in the site header.

C · Inline sentence

Founded in 2021, DripFunnel is part of the Softobotics Group — which means a 40-person engineering team sits behind every campaign we run.

For About pages, bios and proposals, where the relationship is a credibility argument rather than a legal note. Orange-deep link with an orange underline, never brand orange as the text colour.

Where each one goes
  • Website header — DripFunnel lockup only, no endorsement.
  • Website footer — treatment B.
  • About page and case studies — treatment C.
  • Decks, proposals, invoices, email signature — treatment A.
  • Job posts and legal documents — full entity name, no logo.
Don't
  • Don't place the Softobotics logo beside ours, or build a combined mark.
  • Don't set the descriptor larger than 12px or in Manrope — it must never compete with the lockup.
  • Don't write “by Softobotics” or “a Softobotics venture”. The approved wordings are “Part of the Softobotics Group” and “a Softobotics Group company”.
  • Don't put the endorsement in the header, in ads, or on the favicon.
