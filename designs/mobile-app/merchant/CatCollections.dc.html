Choices or filters?
Choices — the shopper picks one when buying, like Size M. Each has its own price and stock. Set them on the product.
Filters — the shopper ticks boxes to narrow down, like Fabric: Linen. Nothing to pick at checkout.
Internal tags — only you see them, like “Reorder soon”.
Tap a value to rename or delete it. Filters also power automatic collections.
