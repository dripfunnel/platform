How a support session works
  • Support opens one only with a reason or a ticket number, and only while this is on.
  • It’s read-only and ends after 30 minutes. Everyone signed in sees a banner while it’s open.
  • You’re emailed when one starts. You or a manager can let them make changes for that one session.
  • Even then they can’t change passwords, payment details, payouts or who works here.
  • Every page they open and everything they change is in your Activity log, under their name.
Separately, DripFunnel’s own staff can sign in as a user to fix a problem. That’s rare, and logged too.
